Lash care

Make your set last.

Six habits we ask every lash client to keep, so a set looks good until the next fill.

Keep them cleanCleanse daily with a lash-safe cleanser to remove dirt, oil and makeup.
Brush dailyA clean spoolie keeps lashes fluffy and in place.
Skip oil-based productsOil breaks down the adhesive. Choose oil-free makeup removers.
Be gentleAvoid rubbing or pulling. Treat them gently and they’ll stay beautiful longer.
Sleep smartSleep on your back or on a silk pillowcase to reduce friction.
Book regular fillsEvery 2 to 3 weeks keeps a set looking full and flawless.
